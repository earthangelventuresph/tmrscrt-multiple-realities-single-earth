## Caption
Figure 1. Emblem of the theory: One Earth, One Clock Time, Many Experienced Reality States. The central sphere is the single Earth and the single physical world Ω. The clock face on the outer ring is the one physical clock time t shared by all observers. The six inner nodes R1 to R6 are observer reality states positioned around the same Earth; each is joined to the Earth by a line, which is its representation map ρi. The dashed ring marks that these states are experiential, while the solid rings are physical.
