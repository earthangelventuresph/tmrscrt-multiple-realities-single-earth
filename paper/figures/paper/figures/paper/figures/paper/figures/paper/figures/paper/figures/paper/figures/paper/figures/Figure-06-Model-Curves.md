## Accurate specification
- Computed from Eq.6 with b0 = 0
- No experimental data
- Panel (a): B plotted against F, for three illustrative values of κ
    - X-axis: F in Hz
    - Y-axis: B in ms
    - Curve falls as F rises
    - κ values are illustrations only. κ is estimated in EXP-1, not assumed.
- Panel (b): Contrast between +2 Hz shift and −2 Hz shift in alpha frequency

    - Contrast is always negative — signed prediction of Postulate 4

## Caption
Figure 6. Model-implied binding window and the EXP-3 contrast, computed from Eq.6 with b0 = 0. Panel (a) plots B against F for three illustrative values of κ. Panel (b) plots the contrast between a +2 Hz and a −2 Hz shift in alpha frequency, which is the quantity EXP-3 manipulates. The contrast is always negative, which is the signed prediction of Postulate 4. The values of κ are illustrations; κ is estimated in EXP-1 and is not assumed.
