# Figure 9 — Decision Logic
**File:** Figure-09-Decision-Logic.png
**Title:** EXP-1, EXP-3, EXP-4 chains
**Project:** EAV-MRCT-001 | TMRSCRT v1.0

## Accurate structure
- Three chains: EXP-1 chain, EXP-3 chain, EXP-4 chain
- Each chain: Left to right, preregistered tests → Terminal box
- Every possible result ends at a terminal box
- Terminal outcomes only:
    - Supported
    - Falsified
    - Uninformative
    - Not supported
- No result is left undecided

## Caption
Figure 9. Decision logic of EXP-1, EXP-3, and EXP-4. Each experiment is a chain of preregistered tests. Following the chain from left to right always ends at a terminal box, so every possible result is classified as supported, falsified, uninformative, or not supported. No result is left undecided.
