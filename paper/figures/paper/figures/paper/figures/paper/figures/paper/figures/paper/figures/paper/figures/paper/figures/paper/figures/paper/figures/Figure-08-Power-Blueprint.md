## Caption
Figure 8. Required sample size for the association test and the equivalence test of EXP-1, from Eq.16 with k = 6. Panel (a) shows that detecting a true partial correlation of 0.20 with power 0.90 needs 265 analyzable participants. Panel (b) shows that rejecting an effect as large as 0.20 with power 0.90, if the true effect is zero, needs 273. The planned 280 analyzable participants satisfy both requirements (power 0.92 and 0.91 respectively).
