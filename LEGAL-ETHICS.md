Purpose, procedures, duration, risks (EEG non-invasive, flicker discomfort/headache, time), benefits (none direct, contribution to science), voluntary, withdrawal anytime without penalty, pseudonymization, data sharing (open data except identifying info), contact, right to results summary, debrief (hypothesis disclosed).

## Participant Protection
Exclusions: Karolinska sleepiness ≥8 (all exps), photosensitive epilepsy / migraine history (EXP-3), neurological condition, psychoactive medication, uncorrected vision/hearing.
Stop rules: Discomfort, dizziness, headache, visual disturbance → stop immediately, record adverse event, offer withdrawal, provide care.
No deception about risks. Hypothesis withheld until debrief only, with ethics approval.
