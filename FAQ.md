## 1. Is TMRSCRT proven?
No. TMRSCRT is a hypothesis and experimental protocol document v1.0. No empirical data have been collected yet. No results are claimed.

## 2. Is this parallel Earths / multiverse?
No. TMRSCRT rejects parallel Earths. One Earth, one Ω, many Ri. Layers are not needed. Realities are observer states.

## 3. Is this saying time doesn't exist?
No. Felt time is constructed by the brain and can differ from clock time. Clock time is physical. Paper does NOT claim physical time does not exist.

## 4. Can I perceive future events before they happen?
No. Φi is prediction, not precognition. Paper explicitly does NOT claim precognition. Λi is anticipation lead, not seeing future.

## 5. What is consciousness frequency?
Fi = Individual Alpha Frequency, Hz. Peak frequency 7-14 Hz of resting eyes-closed EEG over O1, O2, Oz, PO3, PO4, POz. Not vibration. Not a metaphor.

## 6. What is Dij?
Jensen-Shannon divergence between two observers' predictions, in bits. Range 0-1. 0 = identical predictions, 1 = maximum, certain about different events.

## 7. What is Bi?
Temporal binding window. JND of audiovisual TOJ, ms. Bi = b0 + κTi. Higher Fi = narrower Bi = finer resolution.

## 8. What is δi?
Temporal offset. PSS of audiovisual TOJ, ms. Estimated present t̂i = t + δi.

## 9. What is Φi?
Represented-future set. Events with pi(e) ≥ 0.50 within next H = 2 s.
