## Mission
To test extraordinary ideas with ordinary rigor — preregistration, falsification, open data, independent replication.

## Vision
A world where consciousness science is testable, not just discussable. Where every claim has a number, a test, and a way to fail.

## What We Do
- Theory development with closed mathematical models
