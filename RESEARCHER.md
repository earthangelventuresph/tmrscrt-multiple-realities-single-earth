## Research Position
### What Is Asserted in TMRSCRT:
- That multiple realities can be defined as different observer reality states Ri from same Ω, without different Earths.
- That consciousness frequency can be defined as Fi (Individual Alpha Frequency) in Hz, measured from resting EEG.
- That Bi = b0 + κTi, higher Fi = finer temporal resolution.
- That Dij in bits measures reality divergence between observers.
- That P6 physical coupling is testable vs atomic clock at ±1×10-12, and a priori expected to be null.

### What Is NOT Asserted:
- That any experiment has been performed.
- That alpha causes physical time change.
- That observers can perceive events before they happen. Precognition is NOT claimed.
- That consciousness = alpha frequency. Alpha is a proxy, not identity.
- That more than one Earth exists.
- That physical time does not exist.
- That any product, training, or flicker gives access to dimensions.

## Approach
Define every term in units. Every postulate has a test. Every test has a decision for every outcome. Experiential Tier and Physical Tier separate. Null is informative. No p-hacking. No post-hoc thresholds.
