## What We Are Funding
Not a belief. A test.
TMRSCRT is a falsifiable theory with 4 experiments, each with pre-decided threshold that would reject it. Funding supports the tests, not a predetermined outcome.

## What Funding Covers
### Priority 1: EXP-1 — Alpha & Temporal Resolution
- EEG lab access, 280 analyzable participants, participant compensation, preregistration + open data
### Priority 2: EXP-2 — Information Divergence
- 60 observers, testing space, analysis + open data
### Priority 3: EXP-3 — Causal Modulation
- Flicker setup, 130 analyzable participants, safety screening, participant compensation
### Priority 4: EXP-4 — Physical Chronometer
- Atomic clock time, 90 sessions, environmental monitoring, replication with different clocks/building

## What Funding Does NOT Cover
- Personal expenses unrelated to research
- Purchase of dimensions, portals, healings, spiritual services
- Any product claiming to give access to dimensions or precognition
- Any activity violating ethics approval
